AI Pop Optimizer - All in One (v0.4.0, Stellaris 4.5)
=====================================================

Chto delaet mod
---------------
Edinyy performance-mod, kotoryy ogranichivaet rost popov u II i pri etom
NE OSLABLYaET II (glavnaya oshibka suschestvuyuschih modov):

1. Kep popov na koloniyah II. Pri prevyshenii - rost organiki
   (bonus_pop_growth_mult) i sborka (planet_pop_assembly_mult)
   zamorazhivayutsya; pri padenii nizhe kepa zamorozka snimaetsya
   avtomaticheski (v otlichie ot staryh modov, gde zamorozka byla vechnoy).
2. Kompensatsiya ekonomiki II: v zavisimosti ot chisla koloniy II poluchaet
   tirovyy bonus (+10/20/30%): pop_workforce_mult (kazhdyy pop dayot
   bolshe workforce) + country_*_produces_mult po klyuchevym resursam
   (eda, mineraly, energiya, potrtovary, splavy, edinstvo).
3. Ksenosovmestimost: II bolshe ne beryot etot perk (ai_weight = 0).
   Igroku perk polnostyu dostupen. V 4.0 gibridov bolshe net, no perk
   dayot II vzryvnoy rost popov - istochnik lishney nagruzki.
4. Limit sub-vidov II: vanilnoe pravilo can_modify_species
   uzhestocheno (3->2 i 6->4 sub-vida). Menshe subvidov = menshe
   pop-grupp = menshe lagov. Igrok ne zatronut.
5. Filtr spama habitatov: pereopredelyaem vanilnyy script_value
   ai_habitat_cap (daungreyd x0.5) - vanilnaya logika skeyla ot
   razmera galaktiki i slozhnosti sohranyaetsya, Void Dwellers
   sohranyayut dvoynoy kep. Sovmestimo s modami na habitaty.
6. Proverki raspredeleny: ezhemesyachnyy puls tolko po koloniyam II,
   pereschyot kompensatsii - raz v god.

Vazhno pro masshtab 4.0
---------------------
Popy v 4.x masshtabirovany x100. Kep po umolchaniyu 20000 ~ 200 popov
staroy (3.x) sistemy na planetu. Perekalibrovka - odna tsifra v
common/scripted_triggers/apo_triggers.txt.

Pochemu eto uskoryaet igru
------------------------
Po dev diary #370/#372 lag v 4.x porozhdayut POP-GRUPPY
(vid x strata x etika x fraktsiya), a ne otdelnye popy. Sokraschaya obschee
chislo popov II (ih v galaktike bolshinstvo), my sokraschaem i chislo
grupp, i obyom raschyotov workforce. Ksenosovmestimost v 4.0 bolshe ne
sozdayot gibridov, poetomu etot punkt staryh performance-gaydov
ustarel i ne trebuetsya.

Nastroyka
---------
- Kep popov: common/scripted_triggers/apo_triggers.txt (count > 20000)
- Bonusy kompensatsii: common/static_modifiers/apo_static_modifiers.txt
- Porogi koloniy: common/scripted_effects/apo_effects.txt (3/10/20)

Sovmestimost
-------------
- Tolko odinochnaya igra (v MP bonusy II nesimmetrichny).
- Sovmestim s AI-modami (Starnet, Glavius): mod ne pravit AI-vesa,
  tolko rost popov i modifikatory ekonomiki.
- Rabotaet na suschestvuyuschih seyvah (primenyaetsya pri pervom pulse).

Verifikatsiya
-----------
Vse imena triggerov/effektov/modifikatorov svereny s
avtogenerirovannymi logami klienta 4.5.1
(OldEnt/stellaris-triggers-modifiers-effects-list). Povedenie v zhivoy
igre ne testirovalos: posle pervogo zapuska proverte
Documents/Paradox Interactive/Stellaris/logs/error.log na upominaniya apo.

Istoriya versiy
--------------
0.4.0 - Ksenosovmestimost otklyuchena u II (ai_weight = 0) + limit
        sub-vidov II uzhestochyon (3->2, 6->4) v can_modify_species.
0.3.0 - Filtr spama habitatov: override ai_habitat_cap (x0.5)
        poverh vanilnoy formuly, bez override faylov habitatov.
0.2.0 - Identifikatory svereny s 4.5.1 (count_owned_pop_amount,
        num_owned_colonies, planet_pop_assembly_mult, pop_workforce_mult);
        uchtyon masshtab popov x100 (kep 20000).
0.1.0 - Karkas: kep popov II + tirovaya kompensatsiya ekonomiki.
